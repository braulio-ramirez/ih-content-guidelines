# Calendar

Members pick a date and time. Spell things out on first exposure in the calendar, then abbreviate in later compact summaries.

## Calendar header (month + year)

Primary scanning anchor. Spell out months in full. Do not abbreviate years.

Do: `August 2026`  
Don’t: `Aug 2026` / `Aug ‘26`

## Column labels (days of the week)

- Abbreviate to 3 letters
- No periods
- ALL CAPS OK

Do: `MON`  
Don’t: `Monday` / `Mon.`

## Day numbers (grid)

- Numerals only
- No ordinal suffixes (`3rd`)
- No leading zeros (`01`)

Do: `8`  
Don’t: `8th` / `08`

## Selected date heading

Confirms the date currently selected. Same first-spell-then-abbreviate idea: this is the first full weekday + month the member sees.

- Spell out the full weekday **or** use `Today` / `Tomorrow` (not both)
- Comma after the weekday or relative descriptor
- Spell out the full month
- Day as a plain numeral (no ordinals)
- Omit the year (already in the header)
- Do **not** combine `Today`/`Tomorrow` with a weekday

Do: `Today, April 3` / `Tomorrow, April 4` / `Wednesday, April 8`  
Don’t: `Today Friday, April 3` / `Wed, April 8` / `Wednesday, Apr 8` / `Wed, Apr 8th, 2026`

## Visit duration label

Hyphenate the duration as a compound modifier before `visit`. `minute` may be abbreviated to `min`.

Do: `15-minute visit`  
Don’t: `15 minute visit`

## Time slot buttons

- Top of the hour includes `:00` (not `5 PM`)
- No leading zeros (`05:30 PM`)
- `AM` / `PM` with no periods, space before the meridian
- Every slot on screen uses the same pattern

Do: `5:00 PM` / `10:30 PM`  
Don’t: `5 PM` / `05:00 PM` / mixing `5:00 PM` with `5:40pm`

## Confirmed appointment summary

Members already saw spelled-out weekdays and months, so compact form is OK.

- Abbreviate weekday and month (3 letters, no periods)
- Space before and after the bullet (`•`)
- Comma between weekday and date
- Omit `at` before the time
- No ordinal suffixes

Do: `4:30 PM • Thu, Jul 31`  
Don’t: `4:30 PM at Thursday, July 31st`
