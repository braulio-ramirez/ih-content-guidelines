# Status card

At-a-glance status of an Included Health service (often started in chat). Inline banner copy is not in this skill yet — keep alerts short and factual until those specs are added. Button labels follow the buttons spec.

## Title

- 2–3 words (5 max)
- Sentence case; capitalize proper nouns and official IH terms (`Care Team`)

Do: `Advocacy request` / `Chat with Care Team` / `Coverage check`

## Summary

- One sentence max
- Neutral, concise, matter-of-fact
- Do not repeat information already in the conversation
- Do not refer to members in the third person

Do: `File an appeal for a denied MRI bill.` / `Verifying coverage for in-lab sleep study.`

Don’t: `Member needs help filing an appeal for the denied MRI bill.`  
Don’t: repeating that the Care Team was already mentioned (`Verifying in-lab sleep study coverage with care team.` when that was just said in chat)

## Status indicator

Always use a pre-approved status. Never invent one.

v1 approved set, taken from the Status Card sticker sheet (replace if an official glossary is added):

- `In progress`
- `Complete`
- `Delayed`
- `Canceled`
- `Follow-up task created`
- `Finding a specialist`
- `Reviewing your request`
- `Connecting you with an Advocate`
- Custom status only when product already defines the string

## Time-bound indicator (wait / ETA)

- Concrete, exact timelines
- Short waits: do not use ranges spanning more than 10 minutes
- Longer waits: do not use ranges spanning more than 2 days
- Update dynamically as wait times change
- Abbreviate months to three letters, no periods

Do: `End of day Aug 20` / `3-5 business days` / `5-10 minutes` / `Less than 5 minutes`  
Don’t: `10-45 minutes` / `1-5 business days`
