# Examples

## Spoken vs clinical

| Don’t | Do |
| --- | --- |
| Have your symptoms improved since our last communication? | Are you feeling better today? |

## Casual tone

| Don’t | Do |
| --- | --- |
| How ya doin? | How are you feeling today? |
| On-call care on your phone—Included. | On-call care on your phone. |

## Gender and familiarity

| Don’t | Do |
| --- | --- |
| Ask his wife to join the visit. | Ask your spouse to join the visit. |
| We know this is scary. | Here’s what to expect at your visit. |

## Emphasis and marketing

| Don’t | Do |
| --- | --- |
| Book now! | Book visit |
| New Year, New You. | Fresh, specific benefit copy — no cliche hooks. |

## Gestures and CTAs

| Don’t | Do |
| --- | --- |
| Click here to schedule | Book visit |
| Tap Submit | Select **Submit** (forms) / use **Send** for messages |
| Toggle location services on | Turn location services on |
| Start | Get started |
| View more | View more providers |
| OK (on terms of use) | Agree |
| Delete (hiding a saved preference) | Remove |
| Sign in | Log in |
| Sign up (create an account) | Create account |

## Links

| Don’t | Do |
| --- | --- |
| Click here | View your visit details |
| Read more | Read about this provider |
