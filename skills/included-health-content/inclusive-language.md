# Inclusive language

Inclusive language is respectful, accurate, and consistent with how the people being discussed prefer to be described. Avoiding exclusion is the minimum, not the goal.

Source: Inclusive Language glossary (Confluence, PD space). The Inclusive Language Guide in Google Drive is not fully covered here yet.

## Principles

- **Use gender-neutral wording by default.** Prefer `spouse`, `child`, `parent`, `sibling`, `person`, and `people`. Use gendered terms only when the member uses them or the content requires them.
- **Use person-first language by default** (`person with epilepsy`). Follow an individual's stated preference.
- **Use the pronouns the person uses.** When their pronouns are not known, use `they/them`.
- **Community-specific terms** (marked \*) are for community-specific communications only. Use broad terms in broad communications.

## Terms to use instead

| Avoid | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Breastfeeding | Nursing, lactation, chestfeeding\* |
| Grandfather, grandmother | Grandparents |
| Father, husband, male partner | Parent, guardian, family member |
| Maternal | Parental |
| Mother, mama | Person giving birth, person in labor, gestational parent\* |
| Motherhood, fatherhood | Parenthood |
| Brother, sister | Sibling |
| Niece, nephew | Niblings\* |
| Pregnant woman, women | Pregnant person, people |
| Postpartum woman, women | Postpartum parent, parents |
| She/her or he/him | They/them (or the pronouns the person uses) |
| Mother or father | Parent |
| Husband or wife | Spouse or partner |
| Marital status | Relationship status (for example: single, married, partnered, widowed, separated, divorced) |
| "What is your sex?" | "What is your gender?" For a clinical or legal need, "What is your sex assigned at birth?" Ask only when the information is needed. |
| "Do you have a boyfriend/girlfriend or wife/husband?" | "Are you in a relationship?" |
| Opposite sex | Different gender |
| Poor, the poor | People with low income, people experiencing poverty |
| The homeless | People experiencing homelessness, people who are unhoused |
| The elderly, old, geriatric (referring to people) | Aging people, aging population |
| Disabled, handicapped | Person with disabilities, person with diverse abilities. Use "accessible" for spaces. |
| Mental retardation | Intellectual disability, developmental disability |
| Epileptic person, ADHD (as a noun) | Person with epilepsy, person with ADHD |
| Drug abuse | Substance use disorder (SUD), or addiction if clinically accurate |
| Drug problem, habit | Use (illicit drugs), misuse (prescription drugs) |
| Addict, junkie, drug abuser | Person with a SUD (in a clinical setting, patient with a SUD) |
| Addicted to [x] | Has [x] use disorder |
| Clean, stayed clean | Substance-free |
| AIDS patient, HIV patient | Person with AIDS, person with HIV, person living with HIV |
| Positives, HIVers, AIDS or HIV carrier | HIV-positive person or people; person or people with HIV or AIDS |
| Clean (re: HIV) | HIV negative |
| To catch HIV or AIDS; to pass on HIV | To be diagnosed with HIV; to acquire HIV; to transmit HIV |

\* Recommended for community-specific communications, not broad communications.

## Terms to avoid

| Avoid | Why and what to say |
| --- | --- |
| Homosexual | Outdated, and viewed by many as a slur. Name the specific orientation (gay, lesbian) or say "sexual orientation." |
| Lifestyle (with sexual orientation) | Pejorative. Say "sexual orientation." |
| Sexual preference, gender preference, preferred pronouns | "Preference" is offensive here. Say "sexual orientation," "gender identity," or "pronouns." |
| Transgendered, a transgender, transgenders | "Transgender" is an adjective, not a noun. Say "transgender person," "person who is transgender," or "transgender people." |
| Transvestite | Outdated and often a slur. Do not use it for transgender people. |
| Transexual | Dated, and often viewed as a slur. Say "transgender." |
| Transgender identity | Say "gender identity." |
| Sex change, sex change operation | Offensive. Say "medical transition" or "gender-affirming procedure." |
| Hermaphrodite | Outdated, and viewed as a slur. Say "intersex." |
| Non-White | Centers whiteness. Name the race you are discussing (Black, Asian, multiracial). |
| Slurs (for example: tranny, homo, dyke, faggot, shemale) | Never use. Some members have reclaimed these terms, but they are not ours to use. |

## Communities

- Use neutral wording for communities. Say "the Black community," not "Black Americans." Many Black immigrants are not citizens.
- Use an official service name only as the name of that service (for example, Black Health). Do not use it to describe people.
- Do not use "Black health solution" or "LGBTQ+ health solution." Say "our Black Health offering" or "our LGBTQ+ Health offering." Disparities are not solved. We are working to make a positive impact.
